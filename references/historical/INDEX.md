| [Gage — Colour and Culture (1993)](gage-colour-and-culture.md) | The landmark art-historical history of Western colour, antiquity→abstraction — the KB's most-cited source, now with its own entry. New-to-KB: the **Gladstone** episode (language ≠ perception; Greeks weren't colour-blind), **Apelles' four-pigment palette**, *disegno vs colore*, Delacroix's palettes, colour–music (Castel), alchemical colour. Scholarly precedent to Fine's critique. | [Archive.org](https://archive.org/details/colourculturepra0000gage) |
